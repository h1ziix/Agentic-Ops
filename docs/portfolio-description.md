## Project description

**Agentic Ops — Agentic AI Sales Operations Platform**

Built a full-stack system that converts natural-language sales goals into auditable workflows for company research, lead qualification, evidence review, personalized outreach, human approval, controlled execution, follow-up preparation and analytics.

**Tech:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Zod, Gemini, optional OpenAI Planner, Tavily, Trigger.dev, Gmail and HubSpot OAuth.

- Implemented a stateful Orchestrator and bounded typed agents with validated outputs, task dependencies, partial-result preservation and safe execution traces.
- Grounded company assessments and outreach in cited evidence, with independent review and immutable workflow-specific research, qualification and strategy snapshots.
- Separated AI proposals from external authority using exact approval revisions, account generations, deterministic execution, fenced attempts and saved-success replay.
- Built encrypted OAuth credential handling and signed background callbacks; database-owned jobs preserve cancellation, retries and fresh approval for every follow-up email.
- Designed workspace-scoped PostgreSQL Intelligence with explicit cohort semantics and nullable usage/cost instead of fabricated metrics.
- Delivered a responsive operational workspace and isolated fictional public demo that requires no credentials, paid calls or external side effects.
